# Lekha Books logo generation

Created on 2026-11-24. The first exploration was discarded; the refined symbol and horizontal logo below are the delivered artwork. No external brand assets were used.

## Initial symbol exploration

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production symbol for the Lekha Books bookkeeping web and mobile apps.
Primary request: design a refined, original geometric ledger-book logo whose open pages integrate a confident check mark. One compact, distinctive symbol only, no lettering.
Style: precise flat vector-like artwork, generous negative space, bold clean silhouettes that read at 24 pixels. The left page subtly suggests an L; the check forms the upward right edge of the right page. Restrained, balanced, practical accounting identity, not a generic stock icon.
Color palette: white ledger pages and mint teal #4DB6AC check, intended to sit on the project indigo #3949AB. Do not include that indigo background in the output.
Composition: square canvas, centered symbol filling approximately 76 percent of the canvas width and height, equal transparent padding. Transparent outside and inside open negative spaces.
Constraints: genuinely transparent RGBA background, no text, no letters, no words, no extra icon, no circles, no bounding tile, no gradient, no glow, no shadow, no texture, no watermark, no mockup. Output one finished icon.
```

## Final symbol refinement

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Edit target: the attached Lekha Books open-book and check symbol.
Redraw this same book-and-check identity as impeccably clean flat geometric logo artwork. Preserve the general silhouette of an open ledger and a prominent rising check on the right. Simplify to just two thick solid pages and the check; remove the extra outlined page layers. Change the white book to solid indigo #3949AB, and use solid teal #00897B for the check. All shape interiors must be uniformly opaque solid color, without holes, speckles, scratches, dirty edges, grain, shading, outlines or gradients. Exact smooth hard vector-style edges, clean rounded joins. Strong readable silhouette for a production app icon. One centered symbol on a square canvas, approximately 76% width with generous equal padding. Transparent background and open negative space. No lettering, no words, no tile, no border, no shadow, no mockup, no texture. Output a finished transparent PNG.
```

## Final horizontal logo

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Input image: the attached indigo and teal open-ledger checkmark is the exact brand symbol to preserve.
Asset type: primary horizontal logo for Lekha Books bookkeeping software, for README and light-background documents.
Create a polished horizontal lockup using this exact symbol on the left and the exact text "Lekha Books" on the right. Keep the symbol silhouette and indigo #3949AB plus teal #00897B colors. Text spelling is L-e-k-h-a space B-o-o-k-s, title case, no punctuation. Modern confident sans-serif typography; Lekha semibold indigo, Books regular indigo, perfectly aligned to the vertical middle of the symbol. The wordmark should feel carefully typeset, with balanced kerning and generous but compact spacing. Wide landscape canvas with approximately 4:1 aspect ratio, closely fitted to the single horizontal logo with small equal transparent margins, no large empty canvas. Crisp flat solid colors and smooth edges. Transparent RGBA background. No tagline, no extra words, no gradients, no shadow, no watermark, no presentation sheet or mockups.
```
